Behind the build

One week,
one question.

What if a task manager helped you choose what to do, instead of just reminding you how much you have left?

Actstride grew from that question into a complete planner with focus sessions, goals and encrypted backup exports. I wanted it to work without an account or a cloud service. The v1.5.0 release is the result of AI-assisted building, testing and refinement.

A few milestones
  • v1.0 A working next-action planner.
  • v1.2.1 Stronger recovery and offline reliability.
  • v1.4 Encrypted backups and clearer workflows.
  • v1.5.0 Better focus starts and a clearer Today workspace.
